### 1. Whitespace Is Not Empty
We don't fill space. We find the whitespace — the gaps where your brand, funnel, and infrastructure don't connect — and we build there.

### 2. One Stack, No Lock-In
Every deliverable runs on FOSS you can host anywhere. Hugo + Caddy + GHL + AI. Move hosts, swap tools, keep your data.

### 3. B2B & B2C Fluency
We build for salons *and* SaaS founders. The patterns are the same: attention → trust → action → retention. The tactics differ.

### 4. Privacy by Default
No Google Analytics. No Facebook pixels. No Cloudflare. Your clients' data stays on your server — or it doesn't exist.

### 4. Done-With-You, Not Just Done-For-You
You get the keys, the docs, the training. We stay for 30 days. Then you own it — or we stay on retainer.
